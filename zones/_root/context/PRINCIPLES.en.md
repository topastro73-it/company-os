# Decision principles

> How we make decisions. The first ones are system principles (valid for every instance);
> add your company-specific principles at the bottom.

## System principles
1. **Customer Backward** — start from the customer and the problem, then the solution. The law
   before any other.
2. **One master per file** — git for the system, Drive for operations; never two sources of truth.
3. **Permissions are real** — access is decided by Drive ACLs, not by a markdown convention.
4. **Immutable decisions** — a recorded decision is not modified: it is superseded by a new one.
5. **Never promise without validating** — no dates without the engineer, no features without
   product, no numbers without finance, no compliance without evidence.
6. **Decisive, traceable, coordinated** — clear recommendations (not just analysis), every
   output is a file in the right zone, explicit handoffs.
7. **Protect integrity** — of the product, the strategy, the data and privacy.
