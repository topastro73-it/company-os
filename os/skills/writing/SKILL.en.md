# Writing Skill

Writing style for all company content.

## Tone of Voice

> ⚠️ CUSTOMIZE THIS FILE with your brand.

### Principles
- **Clear** — Short sentences. Simple words. No unnecessary jargon.
- **Direct** — Get to the point. The reader has little time.
- **Human** — Write as you would talk to a smart colleague. Not like a press release.
- **Confident but not arrogant** — You know what you're doing, but you don't belittle others.
- **Value-oriented** — Every sentence must add value for the reader.

### Do
- Use "you" to address the reader
- Start with the benefit, not the feature
- Use concrete examples
- Numbers and data whenever possible

### Don't
- No empty buzzwords ("leveraging synergies", "paradigm shift")
- No passive sentences when you can use active voice
- No paragraphs > 4 lines
- No "we are the market leader" without proof

### Formatting
- Clear, descriptive headers
- Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences)
- Bullet points only when needed (not as a default)
- Bold for key concepts, not for decoration

## Anti-AI-Slop Rules

⚠️ **Apply to**: blog posts and newsletters (`company/marketing/blog-posts/`), outbound email and ABM sequences (`company/marketing/`).

### 8 operating rules (summary)
1. **Cut the filler** — no throat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches, adverbs
2. **Break formulaic structures** — no binary contrasts, negative listings, rhetorical setups, false agency
3. **Active voice** — a human subject doing something. No "the decision emerges"
4. **Be specific** — name the exact thing. No "the reasons are structural" or "every/always/never" thrown around loosely
5. **Put the reader in the room** — "you" beats "people"; concrete beats abstract
6. **Vary the rhythm** — mix sentence lengths. Two items beat three. Never em-dashes
7. **Trust the reader** — no softening, justifications, hand-holding
8. **Cut the quotables** — if it sounds like a pull-quote, rewrite it

### Quick check before publishing
- Adverbs? → kill them
- Passive voice? → find the actor
- Inanimate thing with a human verb? → name the person
- Sentence opening with a Wh- word (What/When/Where/Why/How)? → restructure
- "Here's what/this/that"? → get to the point
- "Not X, it's Y"? → say Y directly
- 3 consecutive sentences of the same length? → break one
- Em-dash? → remove it
- Vague declarative ("the implications are significant")? → name the specific thing
- Narrator-from-a-distance ("nobody designed this")? → put the reader in the scene
- Meta-joiner ("in the rest of this article")? → delete

### Scoring (1-10 per dimension)
| Dimension | Question |
|-----------|---------|
| Directness | Statements or announcements? |
| Rhythm | Varied or metronomic? |
| Trust | Respects the reader's intelligence? |
| Authenticity | Sounds human? |
| Density | Anything cuttable? |

**Below 35/50 → mandatory revision.**
